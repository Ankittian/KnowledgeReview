2. COMPREHENSIVE COVERAGE: ALL FORMS IN THE MODULE
================================================================================
Curate cards for ALL active forms in the target module, plus the module macro-overview and major flows.

Typical card allocation (adjust as the content requires):
- Standard / utility forms
- Intermediate transaction / master forms 
- Core transaction anchors (e.g. Purchase Order, Material Receipt Note, Indent, Gate Entry, RFQ, Quotation, CS, Material Issue, Inspection Entry)
- Module-level and business-concept cards
- Business-process cards

This ensures a smooth, well-balanced distribution across the entire module:
- ~35–45% Easy cards 
- ~35–45% Medium cards 
- ~15–25% Hard cards 
These percentages are targets. If the source material supports a different split without losing depth, prefer the better-fitting split and keep the level-to-source emphasis above.

================================================================================
3. AUDIENCE CONTRACT: FUNCTIONAL ONLY
================================================================================
Every flashcard must be written strictly for the business user (Storekeepers, Buyers, Quality Inspectors, Accounts Payable, Plant Supervisors).

Display titles only: Use clear business names (e.g. "Purchase Order", "Material Receipt Note (GRN)", "Gate Entry", "Request for Quotation").

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN IN FUNCTIONAL CARDS:
- NO form codes (fPurchaseOrder, fGRN, fIndent, etc.).
- NO table, view, or schema names (Purchase.PMPurchaseOrder, Inventory.IGRNMain, etc.).
- NO database/programming terms (stored procedure, sp_, SQL, query, method, API, backend, column, dataset).
- NO UI mechanics (dropdown, text box, check box, grid, popup, tab, button, icon, toolbar).
- NO internal rule or message IDs (BR-05, MSG-12, etc.).
- NO field lengths or formats ("(50)", "max 1000 characters") or keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+Y).

================================================================================
4. FLASHCARD VERIFICATION FORMAT
================================================================================
Each flashcard is an educational STATEMENT TO VERIFY (teaching how the ERP functions and confirming business rules):
- `topic_key`: Unique kebab-case slug within the form/scope.
- `title`: Concise topic title (8–110 characters can be a question).
- `body`: plain sentences and bulleted if required segregated by /n .
- `source_refs`: Array of strings pointing to source in the kb folder(form name/module name /BR/process ) (e.g. ["purpose", "validations", "rule:approved-vendor"]).

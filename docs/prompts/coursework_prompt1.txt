A Neo4j knowledge graph containing relationships between forms, database tables, columns, and stored procedures.
Your Task
Generate a structured micro-learning training program for the following form:
Form Name: GRN (Goods Receipt Note)

Instead, divide GRN into multiple meaningful learning sessions.

Each session should teach one coherent subject in sufficient depth.
Avoid splitting a meaningful business process into unnecessarily tiny sessions.
Content Quality Rules
Teach the business concept before explaining the technical implementation.
Explain WHY a business step exists, not just HOW to perform it.
Use realistic manufacturing and procurement examples.
Explain dependencies between fields and processes.
Use simple language understandable to a new ERP employee.
Retain actual Spectrumerp terminology.
Never invent functionality, fields, validations, statuses, or stored procedures.
Distinguish clearly between verified product behavior, inferred behavior, and missing information.
Reference the source document or graph entities wherever possible.
Highlight questions requiring confirmation from an experienced functional consultant.
